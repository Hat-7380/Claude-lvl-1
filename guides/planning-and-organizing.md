# 🗓️ Planning and organizing

Claude is great at turning a messy pile of thoughts into a clear plan. You bring the details, it brings the structure.

## 1. Brain dump → to-do list

Just type everything in your head, messy is fine:

```
Here's everything on my mind, it's a mess:
need to pay internet, mom's birthday sunday, finish report by
thursday, gym?, call bank about card, buy groceries, reply to
Akmal, dentist appointment sometime, clean room

Turn this into a clear to-do list for this week, sorted by
priority. Tell me what to do TODAY.
```

## 2. Plan your week

```
Help me plan my week. Fixed things:
- Work 9:00–18:00 Mon–Fri
- English class Tue and Thu 19:00–20:30
- Family dinner Sunday

Things I want to fit in: gym 3 times, 30 min reading daily,
finish an online course (about 5 hours left).

Make a realistic schedule as a table. Don't overload me.
```

**"Don't overload me"** matters. Without it, AI plans tend to be too ambitious.

## 3. Plan a trip

```
Plan a 4-day trip to Samarkand for 2 people in May.
Budget: medium. We like history and food, not shopping.
We don't want to rush. Give a day-by-day plan with rough costs.
```

Then:
- `Ask me questions first if you need more info.`
- `Make day 2 more relaxed.`
- `Make a packing list.`

⚠️ Check opening hours, prices, and transport times yourself. These change, and Claude may have old info.

## 4. Make a budget

```
I earn [amount] per month. Fixed costs: rent [x], transport [x],
phone [x]. I want to save [x] per month for [goal].
Make a simple monthly budget with categories. Be realistic.
```

Want a spreadsheet? Ask: `Make this as a table I can paste into Google Sheets.`

## 5. Break down a big goal

Big goals feel impossible until they're broken into small steps.

```
My goal: get an IELTS score of 7.0 in 4 months. Current level
is about 6.0. I can study 1 hour on weekdays.

Break this into monthly goals, then weekly tasks for the first month.
```

Works for anything: learning to code, losing weight, saving for a car, writing a thesis, starting a small business.

## 6. Make a decision

When you're stuck between options:

```
I'm deciding between [option A] and [option B].
Here's my situation: [details].
What matters most to me: [list].

Don't decide for me. Help me think it through: list pros and cons,
point out anything I might be missing, and ask me the questions
that would help me decide.
```

## 7. Plan an event

```
I'm organizing a birthday party for my 6-year-old. 15 kids,
at home, 3 hours, small budget. Give me a timeline for the day,
a shopping list, and 5 simple game ideas.
```
