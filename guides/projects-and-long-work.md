# 🗂️ Projects and long work

Normal chats are great for quick things. But what about work that lasts weeks, like a thesis, a business plan, a book, or a job search? You don't want to explain everything again in every new chat.

## The problem

Each new chat starts with **no memory** of your other chats. So you end up pasting the same background again and again.

## Solution 1: Projects (on claude.ai)

**Projects** let you group chats together and give them shared information.

### How to set one up

1. On [claude.ai](https://claude.ai), find **Projects** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create project** and give it a name (e.g. "Master's thesis")
3. Add **instructions**: things Claude should always know or do in this project
4. Add **files**: documents Claude can use in every chat in this project
5. Start chats **inside** the project

> Projects may depend on your plan, and features change over time. If you don't see Projects, use Solution 2 below.

### Example: thesis project

**Instructions:**
```
I'm writing a master's thesis in economics about remote work and
productivity in Central Asia. My supervisor prefers APA style and
short, clear sentences. English is my second language.

When I share writing, give feedback first. Don't rewrite unless
I ask. Always point out claims that need a source.
```

**Files:** your thesis outline, key papers, your supervisor's feedback, your university's formatting guide.

Now every chat in this project already knows all of this.

### More project ideas

| Project | Instructions | Files |
|---------|--------------|-------|
| Job search | Target roles, your strengths, tone | CV, job postings |
| Small business | What you sell, customers, brand voice | Price list, past posts |
| Learning a language | Your level, goals, correction style | Vocabulary lists |
| Home renovation | Budget, style, timeline | Room photos, quotes |

## Solution 2: A "context note" (works everywhere)

Keep a note on your computer or phone with everything Claude needs to know about a big task. Paste it at the start of any new chat.

```
CONTEXT
- What I'm working on: [project]
- Goal: [what success looks like]
- Audience: [who it's for]
- Style: [tone, length, rules]
- Progress so far: [what's done]
- Today I want to: [today's task]
```

Update the "progress so far" line as you go.

## Solution 3: Ask for a summary before you leave

When a long chat is getting slow or you're done for the day:

```
Summarize this whole conversation so I can paste it into a new
chat and continue. Include decisions we made, what's done, and
what's next.
```

Paste that summary into a new chat tomorrow.

## Memory and personal preferences

Claude may also offer settings that let it remember things about you across chats, and a place in **Settings** to describe yourself and your preferences (see [Getting started, Step 5](../start-here/02-getting-started.md#step-5-one-setting-worth-changing)). Check your settings to see what's available on your account and to review or turn off what it remembers.

## Tips for long work

- **One topic per chat.** A chat about chapter 2 and a separate chat about chapter 3 works better than one giant chat.
- **Long chats get worse.** If answers start getting confused or repeating, start a new chat with a summary.
- **Save your best outputs** somewhere outside Claude (a document, notes app). Don't rely on chat history as your only copy.
