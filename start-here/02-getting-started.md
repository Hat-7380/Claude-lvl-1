# 2. Getting started

## Step 1: Create an account

1. Go to **[claude.ai](https://claude.ai)**
2. Sign up with your email or Google account
3. Verify your email if asked
4. That's it. You land on an empty chat box.

## Step 2: Pick where you'll use it

Claude works in several places. Your account and chats are the same everywhere.

| Where | Best for |
|-------|----------|
| **Website** ([claude.ai](https://claude.ai)) | Starting out, long work, uploading files |
| **Phone app** (iOS / Android) | Quick questions, voice, taking a photo and asking about it |
| **Desktop app** (Mac / Windows) | Keeping it open all day next to your other work |

Tip: install the phone app too. Being able to snap a photo of a menu, a letter, or an error message and ask "what does this mean?" is one of the most useful things you can do.

## Step 3: Free or paid?

**Start free.** The free plan is enough to learn everything in this repo.

The free plan has a **usage limit**: after a certain number of messages, you'll be asked to wait a few hours. Paid plans give you much more usage, access to the most powerful models, and extra features.

Upgrade only when you notice yourself hitting the limit regularly. Check [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) for current plans and prices, because they change.

## Step 4: Learn the screen

You only need to know a few things:

- **The message box** at the bottom. Type here. Press Enter to send (Shift+Enter for a new line).
- **The paperclip / + button.** Attach files, photos, or screenshots.
- **New chat.** Starts a fresh conversation with no memory of the old one.
- **Chat history** (sidebar). All your old chats. You can rename, search, or delete them.
- **Model picker** (sometimes shown). Lets you choose which version of Claude to use. If you don't know, leave the default.

## Step 5: One setting worth changing

Open **Settings → Profile** and look for a box where you can tell Claude about yourself and how you like answers. Fill in a few lines, for example:

```
I'm a university student in Tashkent studying economics.
English is my second language, so please use simple, clear English.
I prefer short answers unless I ask for detail.
```

Now every new chat starts knowing this. (If you don't see this option, skip it. It's nice, not necessary.)
