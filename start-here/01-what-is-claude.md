# 1. What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant made by a company called **Anthropic**. You type (or talk) to it in plain language, and it writes back.

That's it. No commands to memorize, no special syntax. If you can send a text message, you can use Claude.

## What it's actually good at

| Good at | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Writing and rewriting** | "Make this email sound more polite" |
| **Explaining things** | "Explain how a mortgage works like I'm 15" |
| **Summarizing** | "Here's a 20-page PDF. What are the 5 main points?" |
| **Brainstorming** | "Give me 10 birthday gift ideas for my dad who likes fishing" |
| **Planning** | "Make me a 4-week plan to start running" |
| **Reading files and images** | "What does this chart show?" (with a screenshot attached) |
| **Translating** | "Translate this into simple Russian" |
| **Coding** | "Write a script that renames all my photos by date" |
| **Thinking out loud with you** | "I'm deciding between two job offers, help me think it through" |

## What it is *not*

- **Not a search engine.** It doesn't always know today's news or prices. Some versions can search the web, but don't assume it.
- **Not always right.** It can state wrong things very confidently. More on this in [Limits and safety](05-limits-and-safety.md).
- **Not a person.** It doesn't remember you between chats unless you use features made for that, and it has no feelings to hurt. You can be direct.
- **Not a doctor, lawyer, or accountant.** It can help you *understand* things and prepare questions, but get real advice for real decisions.

## A good way to think about it

> Imagine a very well-read assistant who started working with you **today**. They're fast, smart, and eager to help, but they know nothing about you, your job, or your situation until you tell them.

That one idea explains most of what makes Claude work well or badly. The more you tell it, the better it gets.

## Claude vs ChatGPT vs others?

They're similar tools from different companies. Most tips in this repo work with any of them. People often say Claude is especially good at writing that sounds natural, long documents, and careful reasoning, but the best way to know is to try the same question in each one.
